What am I looking at?

This is a fisheye view of the whole sky with the zenith at the centre and the horizon at the rim. Rayleigh scattering polarises light most strongly 90° away from the light source, which makes the bright band. The small lines show the direction the electric field vibrates. Bees and some birds use this pattern to navigate.

The model uses single scattering, P = Pmax·sin²γ / (1 + cos²γ). The real sky also has multiple scattering and aerosols, which lower the peak and add neutral points (Arago, Babinet, Brewster). Measuring those differences is part of my research.
